Title:Certifying copies of passport pages
Category: Passport
Language: en
Service: Certifying Passport
Keywords: certification, passport, LibanPost, Lebanon, Lebanese
Content:

In order to get one or more pages of his passport certified, the concerned citizen must show up at the competent general security regional office, having his passport in hand as well as a photocopy of the latter, including the pages he wishes to certify, and a tax stamp of LBP 1000.
The concerned citizen could convey the above-mentioned request at the Lebanese Passports Department, a regional department or LibanPost company.
