Title: Lost Passport / Stolen Passport
Category: Passport
Language: en
Keywords: loss, stolen, passport, minor, public figure, Lebanon, Lebanese
Service: Lost or Stolen Passport
Content:
Lost Passport:

Citizens must declare the loss of their passport in person at a regional office of general security, having in hand documents of identification
If the citizen is a minor, his legal guardian must declare the loss, having in hand documents of identification that underline his appointment as a legal guardian. If the minor is aged 15 years or less, his presence is a must, in such as case.
If the concerned party is a public figure whether political, spiritual, or a military entity, the declaration can be made by an appointed delegate, having in hand an authorization issued by the Operation Room.

Stolen Passport:

The same above-mentioned procedures are applied for stolen passports, with the exception of an additional document required:
A certified copy of the investigation report done by the ISF internal security forces\

NB: The general directorate of General security informs citizens of the necessity to declare a lost/stolen passport even if they don’t intend to apply for a new one.
